Privacy policy

The OAuth client "Home Assistant" is used only by its owner for their own private Home Assistant installation.

  • Access requested: Google Drive files created by this app only (drive.file).
  • Use: uploading, listing and deleting the owner's own Home Assistant backups. The backups are encrypted before upload.
  • No data is shared with, sold to or accessible by anyone else. Nothing is collected from other people.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
